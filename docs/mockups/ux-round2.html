StaffMint · UI round 2 — before vs after

Design only, nothing in the app has changed yet. BEFORE phones are real screenshots of today's app (12 Oct 2026, demo shop "Sharma Medical Store"). AFTER phones show the proposed change. Yellow numbers point at what changed; the notes under each phone explain why.

Changes are grouped in 3 parts: A · Fix mistakes (things that are wrong or confusing today), B · Make it simpler, C · Hindi polish.

All changes in one list

18 points found by opening every screen of the current app in English and Hindi.

  1. A1 Before the shift, Home says "Shift starts at 9:00 AM" instead of "0 of 9 at work".
  2. A1 The date is shown once (not in the header and again in the date bar).
  3. A1 Tips, invites and approvals become one small "To do" card, so the staff list starts higher.
  4. A1 One word for "not here yet" everywhere: "Not in yet" / "अभी नहीं आए".
  5. A2 Leave text fixed: no "paid leave leaves", no "-2"; says plainly the days become unpaid.
  6. A2 No more "punch"; distance shown once; buttons are the main way (swipe is optional).
  7. A3 Ask Mint opens with example sentences and tappable answers; chips wrap instead of being cut.
  8. A4 After salary is approved, old "Is that right?" warnings disappear; month shown once.
  9. A4 "Edited · version 2" becomes "Changed after approval · old slip kept".
  10. A5 Staff profile uses the same symbols and words as the calendar (no P/L/WO/HD codes).
  11. A5 Salary, shift and joining date are no longer mixed under "Salary".
  12. A6 Ask Mint never covers a Save button: pages get space at the bottom.
  13. B1 Add staff offers 3 clear ways and says what happens next; "Default shift" gets a plain name.
  14. B2 Staff tab: a labelled "+ Add" button; "Show inactive" becomes a small filter.
  15. B3 Timings page shows shifts and next holidays directly; Business details explains each switch and what Save covers.
  16. B4 Staff "My salary": amount shown once, one line per reason, no "−₹0".
  17. B5 Staff profile: no "25% complete" nag, language switch only once.
  18. C1 Hindi mode shows Hindi only (English hint only on main buttons), full month names, no "पंच".
