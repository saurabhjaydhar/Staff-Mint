What a payslip has to do

A payslip is read on a small phone, usually opened from WhatsApp, often in Hindi. It has one job for each person:

👩🏽
Pooja (staff)"How much did I get, and is it right?"
  • Sees the amount in hand first, and whether it's paid.
  • Understands every cut in one sentence ("2 absent days", "advance ₹2,000").
  • If something is wrong, can ask the owner from the payslip, not argue at the counter.
👨🏽‍💼
Rakesh (owner)"Send everyone their slip in one go."
  • Checks totals once, then sends all payslips on WhatsApp in one tap.
  • Sees who received and opened it, and who has no WhatsApp.
  • A clean PDF he can print or give to his CA, with his shop's name on top.

What changes from today's payslip

Net pay ("in hand") is the first and biggest thing, not the last row.
Paid or not, when and how (cash/UPI/bank) is printed on the slip.
One language per person (their app language), not every line in both, so it's half as long.
Attendance as a small calendar strip with words, not a 7-column table.
Each cut explained in one short sentence ("2 days absent: 4 Sep, 18 Sep").
"Something's wrong?" button that sends the owner a message about that payslip.
Phone-sized PDF (fits a WhatsApp preview), still prints cleanly on A4.
Shop name and logo on top; StaffMint small at the bottom.
"Revision 2" becomes "Updated on 5 Oct: bonus added", with what changed.

4 · The PDF payslip

The PDF is what staff keep, show a bank or landlord, and what the CA files. Today it's a wide A4 table with everything in two languages, the total at the bottom and no "paid" information. The new one reads well on a phone and still prints cleanly.

BEFOREToday's PDF (rendered with sample data)

  1. No page margins, so text touches the paper edge.
  2. Net pay is the last row.
  3. Every line is repeated in English and Hindi, plus two explanations: long and hard to scan.
  4. No paid date or method; no employee ID or role.
  5. On a phone it's tiny and half the page is empty.
