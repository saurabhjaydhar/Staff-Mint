StaffMint, made easier to understand — mock-up flows

Design only, nothing in the app has changed yet. Each section is one real task, shown screen by screen in the order a person would tap. Yellow numbers point at what changed; the notes under each phone explain why.

Scroll sideways inside a section to follow the flow. Hindi works the same way: every label below has a Hindi version.

The 6 rules 1 · First day as a new owner 2 · Owner home every day 3 · Fix someone's attendance 4 · Month calendar 5 · Approvals 6 · Staff, partners, managers, CA 7 · Salary day 8 · More & settings 9 · Staff app: check in 10 · Staff app: attendance, leave, salary 11 · Help: tips & Ask Mint
1. Words, not just iconsEvery button that matters has a word under or next to it. No mystery grid or mic icons.
2. Words, not codes"Present", "Late", "On leave" instead of P / L / LV. Where space forces codes, the legend is always on screen.
3. Tappable things look tappableStatuses get a small ▾, dates say "change", cards have a clear button with a verb.
4. One screen, one jobHome shows today. Settings are split into small groups. People things sit together under Staff.
5. Explain the first timeA getting-started checklist for new owners, and 2–3 one-time tips per screen.
6. Less on topOne banner at a time. The trial reminder moves down until its last 3 days.
