English

Before

After

What changes

  1. Title matches the More menu: "Plan & payment" / "प्लान और पेमेंट".
  2. Status card: days left in the trial, the price after it, staff used out of the limit, and one main button.
  3. Monthly / Yearly toggle shows "2 months free"; yearly prices show the per-month price and the saving.
  4. Short plan rows that only list what each plan adds; a single compare table replaces repeated feature chips.
  5. Plans that can't hold your staff are greyed out with the reason.
  6. Downgrade confirmation lists what you lose. Upgrades go straight to payment as today.
  7. New Payments section: next payment, GST receipts, one bill for all businesses (moved here), help on WhatsApp.
  8. "Stop plan" button (the server already supports cancelling), with a note that data stays downloadable for 30 days.
  9. Clearer names: "Ask Mint" instead of "Mint AI assistant", "More branches", "Face check at punch-in".

Needs a small server addition for the receipts list (from Razorpay invoices). Everything else uses data the app already has.
