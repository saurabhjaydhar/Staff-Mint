Before = real screenshots of the app today (More → Plan & payment). After = proposed design, not built yet. Same shop in both: Growth free trial, 9 staff.

English

Before

Says the trial ends on 23 Oct, but not how many days are left, what you pay afterwards, or which plan suits you. Free has a big green "Choose plan" button even with 9 staff.

Before

Four look-alike cards repeat every feature, so the plans are hard to tell apart and the page is about 5 screens long.

1 · Best for you

One recommended plan with plain reasons taken from the shop's own use: "Your 9 staff fit", "You used Face check 85 times". Price per day ("about ₹33 a day"). A cheaper option is shown honestly with what you'd lose.

2 · All plans

Each plan has its own colour and picture, a "who it is for" line, staff limit, price a day, and only what it adds (with pictures). Plans that can't fit you say why.

3 · Compare

One table with a picture per row and a tick per plan in that plan's colour, including Pro's CA login and fast help. Price a day at the bottom; price locked for 2 years.

4 · Keep plan

Before paying, 3 simple steps: ₹0 today, ₹999 on 23 Oct then monthly, GST receipt on WhatsApp. UPI, card or net banking.

5 · Payments

Next payment, GST receipts, one bill for all businesses, help on WhatsApp, and "Stop plan".

6 · Downgrade

Shows the saving and exactly what stops, using the shop's own numbers ("used 85 times").

Built (real app screenshots)

The approved design as built: best plan, all plans, compare, payments, the pay sheet and the downgrade sheet.

How it helps people pick the right plan

  1. The app recommends one plan: the cheapest plan that fits your staff and covers what you actually used in your trial (face check, WhatsApp slips, branches). It says why in plain words.
  2. Honest cheaper option right under it, with what would stop working.
  3. Each plan looks different: its own colour and picture (Free = sprout, Starter = shop, Growth = arrow up, Pro = building), the same everywhere on the screen.
  4. "Who it is for" in one line: "Just starting out", "One shop, pay salary in the app", "Growing shop, uses WhatsApp", "Big business, 30+ staff".
  5. Price a day (₹17 / ₹33 / ₹60) next to the monthly price, which is easier to judge.
  6. Pictures with every feature, so it can be understood without reading much, in English or Hindi.
  7. Questions? Chat on WhatsApp for anyone who still isn't sure. ("Ask Mint which plan" was dropped: the assistant can't answer plan questions yet.)
  8. No surprises when paying: ₹0 today, the date of the first payment, price locked for 2 years, stop anytime.

Needs two small server additions: a "what you used in your trial" count for the recommendation, and the receipts list (from Razorpay invoices). Everything else uses data the app already has.
